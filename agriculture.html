HOW IT WORKS

From Subsistence To Surplus

A year after our training program launched in one community, the local cooperative reported its best harvest in over a decade, enough not just to feed their families but to sell the surplus at market and reinvest in the next planting season.

WHAT WE DO

Our Agriculture Focus Areas

The core activities that make up this program.

Farmer Training

Practical workshops on sustainable techniques, crop rotation, and soil health.

Cooperative Support

Helping farming cooperatives access tools, seeds, and shared equipment.

Irrigation & Equipment

Small-scale irrigation systems and equipment loans to extend the growing season.
