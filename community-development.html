HOW IT WORKS

Development Led By The Community

Every project starts with listening. Community leaders help identify the most pressing need, whether that's a well, a footbridge, or a training program, and our role is to provide resources and expertise alongside their own labor and leadership.

Our Community Development Focus Areas

The core activities that make up this program.

Water & Sanitation

Well construction and sanitation projects designed with community input from the start.

Infrastructure Projects

Small-scale roads, footbridges, and community buildings that unlock local access.

Economic Empowerment

Skills training and micro-grants to help households build sustainable income.
