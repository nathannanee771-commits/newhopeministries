WHERE IT STARTED

A Handful Of Volunteers, One Neighborhood

New Hope Ministries Foundation began as a small church outreach, a handful of volunteers sharing food and prayer with families in one neighborhood. Over the years, that outreach grew into a foundation working across dozens of communities, guided by the same conviction that started it all: every person is worth investing in.

"We didn't set out to build an organization. We set out to sit with families who had nowhere else to turn — the rest followed."

— FOUNDER, NEW HOPE MINISTRIES FOUNDATION
2008
